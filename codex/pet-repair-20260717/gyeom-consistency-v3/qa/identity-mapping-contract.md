# Gyeom identity mapping contract

## Canonical mapping

| Area | Locked appearance | Reject when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Face and hair | Warm clay/toy face, brown eyes, gentle expression, thick tousled black center-parted waves | Face shape, hair volume, eye color, or material changes |
| Proportions | Slim youthful body; stable head-to-body ratio, narrow shoulders, long straight legs | Head, torso, arms, hands, or legs visibly scale differently by row or frame |
| Top | One light-gray heather raglan torso panel; black collar, shoulders, and long sleeves | Hoodie, zipper, jacket, layers, altered panel shape, or palette drift |
| Jeans | One dark indigo-black straight-leg denim pair, subtle copper stitching, cuffed ankles | Blue/light pants, slacks, joggers, shorts, altered seams, or no cuffs |
| Shoes | Charcoal black canvas low-tops, off-white toe caps, white laces | Changed color, silhouette, or footwear type |
| Registration | Shared practical scale, feet baseline, and centered body mass | Floating feet, baseline jump, scale pop, or lateral slide unrelated to the action |
| Edge quality | Transparent silhouette with natural clean pixels | Opaque magenta, red, green, or purple background/outline residue; halos; detached fragments |

## Acceptance rule

Every standard action row and every look-direction row must match every canonical mapping. A visual mismatch in any clothing, proportions, or face/hair identity is a blocker that requires regeneration of the whole containing row before packaging.
